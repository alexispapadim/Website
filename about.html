The chef

I don’t cook to manipulate flavour.I cook to show it off.

For me, it has always been entirely about the produce. My passion begins and ends with it. True cooking isn’t about forced creations — it’s about having the restraint to let the ingredients speak for themselves.

Drawing on my training — from the disciplined precision of Michelin fine-dining kitchens to the honest, real traditions of classic Italian, Greek and Japanese cooking — I focus entirely on bringing out the natural beauty of what is right in front of us.

But above all, my canvas is your vision. It is all about what the guest wants, and it is my job to make it happen — no matter the request, the cuisine or the dietary demand.
